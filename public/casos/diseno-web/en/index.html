UI/UX Web Design

Six industries, one standard

Web design isn't a template you repeat: every industry solves different problems, even as the visual and usability standard stays consistent. A sample of the range of projects.

Moser Café Kultur

2019 · Café with live jazz shows

Website and social media kit for a café that doubles as a live jazz venue — the identity had to hold up both sides of the business, coffee shop by day and venue by night.

Café Amadeus

2019 · Restaurant

Responsive website for a restaurant — the goal is simple but demanding: the menu and location need to look good and read fast, no matter what screen the customer lands on.

Grupo Uno22

2020 · Brand holding company

Corporate website for a holding company that groups several brands under one umbrella — the visual hierarchy has to make clear what the group is and what each brand within it is.

Saxodia

2020 · Saxophone quartet

Website and app for a saxophone quartet — digital presence for a musical act, built to book dates and showcase repertoire.

V-Dental

2021 · Dental supplies

Ecommerce and app for a dental supplies distributor — the most functionally ambitious project of the group: catalog, cart and checkout for B2B buyers, not just a static catalog.

Viterbo 68

2023 · Real estate

Website and app for a real estate developer — the usual real estate challenge: every property's photos and floor plans need to look good and load fast.

Incómodo

2023 · Creative studio

Web portfolio for a creative studio — here the site itself is the piece on display, so the design bar is higher: it has to stand on its own as original work, not just showcase someone else's.
