Hugo González: digital image for a professional goalkeeper

Personal social media branding and digital image for the Mazatlán FC goalkeeper during his time at the club, as part of the Victorem Sports Marketing team — content timed to every relevant match of the season, Liga MX and Leagues Cup.

The brief

Hugo González played as a professional goalkeeper for Mazatlán FC. As part of the Victorem Sports Marketing team, the brief was to maintain his digital image on his personal social media during that time at the club: the player's own branding, not official club content, timed to every relevant match of the season — Liga MX and Leagues Cup, the tournament that pits Mexican and U.S. clubs against each other.

Match by match

Each piece follows the same pattern: the goalkeeper's presence in the match, the result, a recap — repeated against Tijuana, Puebla, Monterrey, Pachuca, Necaxa and Juárez, plus two editions of Leagues Cup.

Why personal branding matters

A professional player isn't just their role on the pitch — their personal brand is part of their commercial value. Sustaining it consistently, match after match across an entire season, is exactly the kind of work a sports agency provides for the athletes it represents.
